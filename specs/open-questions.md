| OQ-IOS-1 | Does iFlytek offer an iOS wake-word SDK compatible with the existing `wakeword.jet` model and app id? If not, which engine and licence? | M5 | PARTIAL → OWNER | **An iOS SDK exists** (`iflyMSC.framework`, `IFlyVoiceWakeuper`, min iOS 8). It loads a `.jet` resource from the bundle (`ivw/<APPID>.jet`), threshold syntax `0:1450` as on Android, `keep_alive=1` for continuous listening. Two limits: the static library is bound to an app id (error 10407 otherwise), and "目前iOS唤醒只支持应用级唤醒" - **in-app wake only**, no system-level wake. So the wake word works only while our app runs (foreground, or background audio session per OQ-IOS-4). Reusing the same `.jet` needs the iOS SDK downloaded from the console for the **same app id**: an owner account action. Source: https://www.xfyun.cn/doc/asr/awaken/iOS-SDK.html |
| OQ-IOS-2 | Amap iOS Navi SDK: multi-route calculation and selection, guidance text callback, mute the built-in voice and speak via the app, camera/speed-limit info, simulated navigation, CarPlay rendering | M3, M4, M7 | ANSWERED | All present in `AMapNaviKit`: **multi-route** `calculateDriveRouteWith…:drivingStrategy:` with `AMapNaviDrivingStrategyMultipleDefault`, routes read from `naviRoutes`; in-guidance alternatives with `setMultipleRouteNaviMode:` (v6.3.0+). **Voice**: `isUseInternalTTS` defaults to **NO** (v5.5.0+); guidance text arrives in `driveManager:playNaviSoundString:soundStringType:` paired with `driveManagerIsNaviSoundPlaying:`, so the app speaks it (our relay). **Camera / speed limit**: `driveManager:updateCameraInfos:` (type, whether it limits speed, coordinates, distance; interval-speed cameras from v6.0.0); `updateNaviInfo:` for road, remaining time and distance. **Simulated drive**: `startEmulatorNavi` after route success ("仅用于室内模拟"). **CarPlay**: Amap publishes a CarPlay adaptation demo for `AMapNaviDriveView` (guidance panel, junction view, overview, pan/zoom). The route-selection call name was not in the pages read; confirm in the API reference during M3. Sources: https://lbs.amap.com/api/ios-navi-sdk/guide/route-plan/drive-route-plan , …/guide/navigation-map/multi-navi-path , …/guide/voice/speech-synthesizer , …/guide/navigation-map/navi-info , …/guide/navigation-map/gps-navi , …/guide/tools/carplay_navi |
| OQ-IOS-3 | Is `AVAudioEngine` voice processing enough to prevent self barge-in with loudspeaker playback in a car, or must WebRTC AEC3 be ported? | M1 | DEVICE_SPIKE | Documented: `AVAudioInputNode.setVoiceProcessingEnabled(_:)` enables Apple's voice processing (echo cancellation) on the I/O node; the reference is the same engine's output, so playback must go through that engine (as the spec already says). Its quality against loud speaker playback is not documented anywhere; only the speech-harness clips on an iPhone answer it. Source: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudioionode/setvoiceprocessingenabled(_:) |
| OQ-IOS-4 | Background policy: may the app keep the mic and WebSocket alive in background (audio background mode) and pass App Review? Screen lock during navigation? | M5 | ANSWERED (review risk stays) | `UIBackgroundModes` declares "services provided by an app that require it to run in the background" (values include `audio`, `location`). App Review Guideline **2.5.4**: "Multitasking apps may only use background services for their intended purposes: VoIP, audio playback, location, task completion, local notifications, etc." Navigation guidance with spoken prompts is a recognised `location` + `audio` use; holding the mic open only to listen for a wake word is not one of the named purposes, so plan: background listening only while a voice session or route guidance is active, mic indicator visible, and describe it in review notes. Amap's own sample sets `allowsBackgroundLocationUpdates = true` for guidance with the screen locked. Sources: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/uibackgroundmodes , https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#2.5.4 , https://lbs.amap.com/api/ios-navi-sdk/guide/navigation-map/gps-navi |
| OQ-IOS-5 | Gemini Live API: is the protocol in contracts/api-contracts.md current? Any iOS SDK? | M1 | ANSWERED | Current. Endpoint `wss://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/ws/google.ai.generativelanguage.v1beta.GenerativeService.BidiGenerateContent` (v1beta); models `gemini-3.8-live`, `gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking` and `gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview` are listed; `audioStreamEnd`, `generationComplete`, `toolCallCancellation`, `goAway`, `sessionResumption` / `SessionResumptionUpdate` and `contextWindowCompression.slidingWindow` are documented. Limits: audio-only session 15 min without compression, connection lifetime ~10 min (so resumption is required, as on Android). For production the docs recommend **ephemeral tokens** instead of a raw API key on the client. iOS SDK: Firebase AI Logic offers the Live API for Swift / Apple platforms; a plain `URLSessionWebSocketTask` client matching the Android protocol is equally valid. Sources: https://ai.google.dev/api/live , https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/live-session , https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models , https://firebase.google.com/docs/ai-logic/live-api |
| OQ-IOS-6 | CarPlay: navigation entitlement eligibility; confirm no vehicle-control access | M7 | ANSWERED (+ OWNER choice) | Every CarPlay app needs a category entitlement requested at developer.apple.com/carplay and reviewed by Apple. **"Multiple categories cannot be combined in a single app"** (except EV charging + fueling): the app must be either a **navigation** app (base view only draws the map, turn-by-turn maneuvers, stop guidance when the car's own navigation starts, prompts mix with car audio) or a **voice-based conversational** app (`com.apple.developer.carplay-voice-based-conversation`, iOS 26.4+; voice is the primary modality, audio session only while voice is active, `CPVoiceControlTemplate`). Not both. The CarPlay framework and guide expose templates only; no climate, seat or window API appears anywhere in them, so vehicle controls stay simulated. Which category to apply for is an owner decision (see OQ-IOS-8). Sources: https://developer.apple.com/download/files/CarPlay-Developer-Guide.pdf (2026-10-13 edition), https://developer.apple.com/documentation/carplay |
| OQ-IOS-7 | Named music: is MusicKit (Apple Music) acceptable as the only named-music path? Netease/QQ Music options? | M6 | OWNER | Documented: MusicKit `ApplicationMusicPlayer` plays Apple Music items inside our app without changing the Music app's state, and keeps playing in background when the app declares the background audio mode. It needs the user's Apple Music subscription. iOS gives no API to drive third-party players (Netease, QQ Music), unlike Android's media-session handoff. Whether Apple Music only is acceptable is the owner's product call. Source: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/musickit/applicationmusicplayer |
| OQ-IOS-8 | CarPlay category: navigation app or voice-based conversational app? (raised by OQ-IOS-6) | M7 | OWNER | Navigation: map and guidance on the car screen, voice via our audio prompts. Voice-based conversational: 小诺 as a voice assistant on the car screen (iOS 26.4+), no map. One app cannot be both. Recommendation: navigation, because the demo's core is guided driving. |
